# BatteryGlance privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

BatteryGlance runs entirely on your PC. It collects no personal data and has no accounts, ads or usage statistics.

**What the app reads:** names and battery levels of your Bluetooth and USB devices, to display them. This data stays on your PC.

**What is stored:** settings (`%AppData%\BatteryGlance`), the battery history of the last 14 days, a diagnostic log and, on request, a diagnostic report (`%LocalAppData%\BatteryGlance`). None of it is sent anywhere. The diagnostic report only leaves your PC if you send it to someone yourself.

**Internet connections:** the version installed with `Setup.exe` checks GitHub for updates every 6 hours and downloads them from there, so GitHub sees your IP address ([GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)). The Microsoft Store version is updated through the Store. The app makes no other internet connections.

**Local connections:** if Logitech G HUB is running, the app asks its service on your own PC (`localhost`) for battery levels. The optional interface for other programs is off until you turn it on and can only be reached from your PC.

To remove all data after uninstalling, delete the folders above.
